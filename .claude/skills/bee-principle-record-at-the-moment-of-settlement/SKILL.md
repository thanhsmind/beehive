# Record at the Moment of Settlement

The trigger is audible. When you hear or say "okay, so we'll do X", write the
record before the conversation moves on. That sentence already IS most of the
record — it is missing only its boundary and its why.

Write it in the same working session, while the alternatives are still sharp. A
cleanup pass at the end reconstructs the decision from memory, and
reconstruction loses exactly the parts that mattered: the boundary cases, the
rejected option, the explicit "and we do NOT do X".

**Why:** the cost asymmetry always points one way. At the moment of settlement
the record costs one line, because everything is already loaded in your head.
Six weeks later the same question costs the whole debate again — often landing
on a different answer, which now silently disagrees with the code built on the
first one.
