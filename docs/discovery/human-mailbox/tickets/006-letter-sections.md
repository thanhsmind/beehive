## Question

What sections does the body carry? `ak:sumup` names nine (implementation,
failures, workarounds, decisions, behavior, architecture, usage,
follow-ups, next steps) — more than a nightly letter should hold. Which
subset, and does a feature-close letter carry more than a nightly one?

➡️ Recommendation: five for the nightly letter — *Done / Where I
departed from the plan and why / Broken or unfinished / Needs your call
/ Next*. Architecture, behavior and usage only appear in the letter
written when a feature closes, where they are actually earned. Every
section that has nothing to report is dropped, not printed empty.

## Answer

Five sections — logged as **D7 (b94381e5)**: Done / Where I departed from
the plan and why / Broken or unfinished / Needs your call / Next. A
section with nothing to report is dropped, never printed empty.
Architecture, behaviour and usage appear only in the letter written when
a feature closes.
