## Answer

Advisor-tier research (dispatch 80389fd8, 2026-08-29):

- (a) Seam = bee-shaping, AFTER the spec content is drafted and BEFORE Lock.
  The five hats ask product-altitude questions, which shaping owns
  exclusively; after Lock a product finding has no channel except
  supersession. Planning's review wave critiques the SHAPE post-Lock (product
  critique is contractually forbidden there); blind lanes' cross-critique is
  round two of a GENERATION run with rivals — hats never see each other and
  have no rivals; bee-reviewing is user-invoked over finished work. Lanes and
  hats end up complementary at one altitude: lanes when no design exists yet
  (high-stakes AND ambiguous), hats when ONE draft exists and is hard to
  reverse.
- (b) Docs-only procedure over the existing dispatch door, no new verb —
  strictly less machinery than blind lanes needed. Five parallel
  `dispatch prepare --kind advisor` dispatches; the perspective rides the
  prompt (NOT --brief-file, so the lane neutrality guard — built for neutral
  briefs — never fights a brief whose leaning IS its job). Nothing here
  autonomously logs a decision, so no checker verb: the human at Lock is the
  check. Discretionary with the reason logged at open (same D1 posture as
  lanes). Procedure text single-homes beside "Blind lanes and convergence"
  in gates-and-delegation.md; bee-shaping carries the one-line trigger
  pointer.
